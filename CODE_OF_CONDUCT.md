# Code of conduct

KanRite follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful, whatever someone's background,
  identity or experience.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. Accept a "no" gracefully, and
  give reasons when you say one.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, personal attacks,
  or publishing someone's private information.

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainers privately through
[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/kanrite/amd/security/advisories/new)
on the Security tab, which only maintainers can read; say it's a conduct
report. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues
that don't follow this code, and may warn, or temporarily or permanently
ban, anyone who doesn't, following the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
